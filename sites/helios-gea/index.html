O namaAbout us

Ideja iz prirodeThe story about dedication to nature

Priča o tvrtki Helios Gea rođena je 2009. godine u Istri, predivnom djeliću Mediterana prošaranom tartufima, vinovom lozom i stoljetnim maslinicima. Upravo s maslinama nama se dogodila ljubav na prvi pogled. Nakon što smo otkrili koliko ljekovitih svojstava sadrži taj maleni zeleni plod, maslina je postala srž naše poslovne ideje u narednim godinama.

The story of Helios Gea company was born in Istria, Croatia, a charming Mediterranean region rich with truffles, vineyards and olive groves. It was these very olives and their medicinal benefits that caught our attention and became the core of our business in the following years.

Održivi razvojSustainability

Mi vjerujemo u dugoročan i harmoničan odnos s prirodom i lokalnom zajednicom te tako zajednički gradimo ispravan put do uspjeha. Stvorivši prirodnu kozmetiku za njegu kože, Helios Gea promovira ekološke inovacije, zdrav život, potrebe i životni stil naših klijenata te proizvode izvrsne kakvoće.

Tijekom proizvodnje koristimo isključivo kominu masline iz Istre koja dolazi s eko farmi te je strogo kontrolirana, a time pomažemo zbrinjavanju komine lokalnim maslinarima i gradimo ekološke vrijednosti u regiji.

We believe in a long-lasting mutual relationship with nature and local community, the only road to success we want to take. Respect towards nature, respect towards our clients and employees, respect towards beauty. By creating natural and chemical-free skin care cosmetics, Helios Gea wants to promote principles of ecological innovation, healthy life, our clients’ needs and products of highest quality.

We are the only company that enables olive producers to productively dispose of olive pulp, thus helping to maintain ecological values in the region. Our goal is to embrace nature, beauty, local community and future innovations in a story worth to be told. And experienced.

InovacijaInnovation

Inovativan način prerade komine masline s primjenom u kozmetičke svrhe Innovative method of processing olive pulp biomass for cosmetic purposes

Zahvaljujući brojnim istraživanjima i mukotrpnom radu pronašli smo inovativan način prerade biomase od komine masline za dobivanje zdravstveno ispravnog aktivnog elementa s primjenom u kozmetičke svrhe. Slijedili smo instinkt i inspiraciju te u isto vrijeme nastojali zadržati skladan odnos s prirodom. Danas s ponosom možemo reći kako smo uspjeli u tome.

Our extensive research and hard work resulted in an innovative procedure for extracting a medically approved active element from olive pulp biomass for cosmetic use. We followed our instinct and inspiration, while using our best efforts to maintain a harmonious relationship with nature. Today, we can proudly say we have succeeded.

45. paralela kao inspiracija za brendThe 45th parallel as the inspiration for the brand

Općinom Bale, odnosno njezinim najjužnijim dijelom prolazi 45. paralela, što znači da se nalazi na najidealnijem mjestu – na pola puta između Ekvatora i Sjevernog pola. Otuda inspiracija za ime linije proizvoda „45 degrees”.

The 45th parallel passes through the small town of Bale and its most southern part, which means halfway between the Equator and the North Pole – the most ideal place to live. Hence the inspiration for the name of the product line “45 degrees”.

Od ideje do gotovog proizvoda From concept to finished product

Helios Gea d.o.o. bavi se razvojem i realizacijom personaliziranih proizvoda prilagođenih potrebama i identitetu naših klijenata.

Helios Gea d.o.o. specializes in the development and production of personalized products tailored to the needs and identity of our clients.

Našim partnerima omogućujemo da svoje ideje pretvore u gotove proizvode brendirane njihovim logotipom i vizualnim identitetom – od odabira proizvoda i sastava, preko ambalaže i dizajna, do finalne proizvodnje.

Proizvodnju realiziramo u suradnji s renomiranim i provjerenim proizvođačima iz Hrvatske i inozemstva. Zahvaljujući širokoj mreži proizvodnih partnera, možemo ponuditi rješenja iz različitih kategorija i prilagoditi ih specifičnim zahtjevima svakog klijenta.

We help our partners turn their ideas into finished products branded with their own logo and visual identity – from product selection and formulation to packaging, design and final production.

We work with a network of renowned and trusted manufacturing partners in Croatia and abroad. Thanks to our extensive network of production partners, we can offer solutions across a wide range of categories and adapt them to the specific requirements of each client.

Our particular strength lies in our ability to combine product development, personalization, packaging, branding and complementary products into one complete solution.

Na taj način možemo kreirati kompletne kolekcije i poklon pakete – od kozmetičkih proizvoda i hotelskih amenitiesa do dodataka za wellness, lifestyle i poslovne poklone – sve vizualno usklađeno s brendom i namjenom proizvoda.

Bilo da je riječ o proizvodima za vlastiti brend, korporativnim poklonima, hotelskoj kozmetici, promotivnim proizvodima ili proizvodima namijenjenima prodaji, naš cilj je ponuditi kvalitetno, funkcionalno i vizualno prepoznatljivo rješenje koje odgovara potrebama i pozicioniranju svakog klijenta.

We can create complete collections and gift sets – from cosmetic products and hotel amenities to wellness, lifestyle and corporate gifts – all visually aligned with the brand and the intended purpose of the product.

Whether you are looking for products for your own brand, corporate gifts, hotel cosmetics, promotional products or products intended for retail, our goal is to provide a high-quality, functional and visually distinctive solution tailored to each client's needs and market positioning.
